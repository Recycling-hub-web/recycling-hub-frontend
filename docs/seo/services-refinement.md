# Services refinement after market research

The user requested researching other businesses and updating the services. This authorizes the service catalogue and its six existing detail pages, their EN/BM metadata and menu/quote labels; other SEO page batches remain pending.

## Research and editorial decision

Reviewed public Malaysian examples: [pickup/business collection and data-destruction presentation](https://erth.app/), [equipment-based recycling and destruction service catalogue](https://recycle.my/services/), and [consumer recycling and rewards programme](https://kitarecycle.com/).

These are presentation references, not evidence of Recycling Hub capabilities. No competitor copy, prices, certifications, equipment categories or operational guarantees were adopted. The retained core capabilities come from the business brief; uncertain delivery details require confirmation before booking.

## Result

Four main service cards: household e-waste pickup, business and bulk collection, certified data destruction, and electronics recycling. Two supporting cards: collection and sustainability records; business collection documentation. Menu labels, quote options and structured data use the same translated card names. The business quote form defaults to bulk collection.

All existing URLs remain unchanged, including `/services/esg-reporting` and `/services/material-recovery`. Their visitor-facing names now describe the narrower service honestly. No redirects or new service pages were introduced.

Removed unsupported service-detail claims about DOE registration, guaranteed recurring schedules, destruction methods/standards, per-device certificates, carbon estimates, zero-landfill results, and guaranteed audit acceptance. Removed unfinished photo/certificate placeholders. No new certifications or numerical environmental outcomes were invented.

Household pickup still requires at least **3 devices**. Contact actions use the established WhatsApp number **+60 11-6080 3604**. Each service has a quote link; household pickup retains its direct pickup action too.
