# Solutions and navigation simplification

The user approved reducing overlap between Services and Solutions, then asked to simplify the presentation further.

## Current presentation

The top navigation has one **Services** menu. It lists the four main services, a link to all services, and **Who we help** linking to `/solutions`. Documentation/reporting support remains on the Services page. No second Solutions dropdown is displayed on desktop or mobile.

Audience pages remain at their existing URLs. Their content now focuses on preparation and audience requirements, with links to relevant Services rather than repeating the service catalogue. No pages or live slugs were deleted or renamed.

Removed unconfirmed company-registration, automatic per-device certificate, procurement-readiness and nonprofit discount claims. Removed testimonial placeholders. All new copy and metadata is in the EN/BM catalogues. Household pickup requires at least 3 devices. WhatsApp remains +60 11-6080 3604.
